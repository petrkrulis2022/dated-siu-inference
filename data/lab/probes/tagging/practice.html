How this works

Each reason below is a line an AI agent wrote about what it was doing in a small market, mostly about how to pay: in USDC (dollars) or in fSIU (a claim for work). A keyword rule, written before any of these reasons were collected, sorts reasons like these into nine categories. You tag a few practice ones by hand, so we can see how far the rule agrees with a person reading the same words. Nobody looks at the rule's answers for these reasons until you have finished, and nothing on this page says which agent wrote what.

  1. Read the reason.
  2. Tick every category that fits. One reason can fit several.
  3. If none fits, tick None of these.
  4. Press Next. Back lets you change an earlier answer.
  5. After the last reason your tags are saved to Claude. You can also download them.

Go by what the reason means, not by whether it contains a particular word. Do not try to guess which agent wrote it or what answer is expected. It takes about 30 minutes. You can close the page and come back: answers are saved as you go and the page resumes at the first reason you have not tagged. Keys 1 to 9 tick a category, 0 ticks None of these, Enter moves on.

The nine categories

  • 1Earmarking
    one asset assigned to a purpose ("preserve X for raw work", "keep X for later needs")
  • 2Price direction
    the print rising, falling, up, down, a trend
  • 3Balance size
    "I have plenty or enough of X", an amount held
  • 4Familiarity
    USDC as simpler, standard, safe or the default
  • 5Following the brief
    the neutrality sentence, the scoring rule, "as stated"
  • 6Keeping options open
    flexibility, liquidity, not committing
  • 7Expiry
    the window closing, fSIU expiring
  • 8Cost or efficiency
    cheaper, minimal, economical
  • 9No asset reason
    the need or the obligation only, nothing about which asset

Number 9 is for a reason that cites only the need or the obligation, such as "to meet my round 2 need", and nothing about why that asset or why now. None of these is for a reason that says something about the choice that fits none of the nine.
