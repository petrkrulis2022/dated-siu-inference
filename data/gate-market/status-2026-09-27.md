## The task

One pinned job: author a hardened *quality gate* (grader) for `dedupeSorted(arr)` — a function
that removes consecutive duplicate values from a sorted array — adversarially resistant to known
cheats (stubbed tests, hardcoded answers, exception swallowing, etc.). ORCHESTRATOR is paid only
if a passing gate is actually delivered before its turn/budget limit. It structurally cannot
author one itself (withheld reference materials, not a rule) and must subcontract to WORKER-CODE,
in USDC or in a minted fSIU work-claim — **which asset it picks when neither is favored is the
actual experiment (spec's F1, "the choice test").**
